# Screenshots for the store pages (1.1.0)

CurseForge and Wago show screenshots in upload order, with the caption under each. Take them in game at 1920x1080 or larger, UI scale around 1.0, with the box (`/tb menu`) centred and nothing else overlapping it. Crop to the box plus a little of the world around it. Save as PNG into `art/screenshots/` with the file names below.

A character with a few sessions of fishing, a lure in the bags and Auctionator prices makes every tab look full.
